Addressing the complexity in the room

Workshop held at the Swiss Public Health Conference 2026, where participants build a map of a health problem together with people who do not think exactly like them.

2 September 2026 Lucerne, Switzerland Swiss Public Health Conference

Organisers

  • Dr. Cornelia Wagner Population Health Lab, University of Fribourg, Switzerland
  • Dr. Adrian G. Zucco Copenhagen Health Complexity Center, University of Copenhagen, Denmark

Material

Guide for participants

The exercise step by step — introductions, picking a problem, brainstorming factors, drawing the arrows, and the closing discussion.
